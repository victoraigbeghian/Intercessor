# Intercessor — WordPress Prayer Request Plugin

Intercessor gives churches, ministries, and faith-based communities a complete prayer request management system built on WordPress.

Visitors submit prayer requests through a front-end form. Site administrators moderate, track, and manage those requests from the WordPress dashboard. Logged-in members can browse approved requests on a Prayer Wall and record that they've prayed, with live counters and optional email notifications to requesters.
